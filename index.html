Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a handyman visit cost?

It depends on the job and how many items are on your list. We look at the work first, give you a price before anything starts, and you decide from there.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The initial consultation and estimate are free.

What kinds of jobs do you take on?

General repairs, drywall patching and painting, carpentry and door work, fixture installation, TV and shelf mounting, furniture assembly, and routine home maintenance. If it's on your list, ask — we'll tell you straight if it's something we handle.

Can I bundle several small jobs into one visit?

Yes, and it's usually the best value. Send the full list and we'll plan the visit around all of it.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. We handle repairs, touch-ups, and turnover work for rental properties and coordinate access with tenants when needed.

What areas do you serve?

Torrance and the surrounding South Bay and Orange County cities, including Redondo Beach, Carson, Long Beach, Seal Beach, and Huntington Beach.
